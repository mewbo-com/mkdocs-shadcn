# Masthead

## The opening H2 is the page's visual title

A page opens as a pair. The H1 above names where the reader is and renders small
in the accent colour. This H2 is the title itself and renders large, set tight
against the label so the two read as one block.

## A real section heading

Every later H2 keeps the ordinary section treatment, with its normal size and
its normal space above it. Only the first one is the title.

### A subsection

Nested headings are untouched.
